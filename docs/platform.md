# Courses — active product direction

Updated 2026-10-02. The owner explicitly authorized the transition from clone finalization into course-selling and community development. Preserve the music reference, existing implementation and honest parity ledger. The product is now developed under `/learn` in the same app; finishing all remaining Apple MATCH/FLOW entries is no longer a prerequisite for this work.

## Product promise to validate
Help people discover a useful course, understand its offer, learn at their own pace, practice and ask for help in context. Use the editorial, content-first quality of the existing Apple Music-derived interface, with independent branding and original/licensed assets. “Better than Skool” is the owner's ambition, not a verified comparative claim or permission to copy a competitor's proprietary content.
The working name is Courses. A marketplace and creator community can share the same learning model; do not turn every subject into a separate specialized application. Coding does not require a built-in IDE, business education does not imply investment services, and fitness does not imply a workout tracker.

## People and jobs
Visitors need truthful outcomes, teacher identity, sample lessons, prerequisites, curriculum, language, effort, price and access terms. Learners need legitimate access, clear saved-versus-started-versus-owned states, reliable resume, readable content, useful notes and contextual answers. Creators need draft authoring, preview, publication, student questions and understandable commerce. Operators need moderation, review, support and attributable access recovery.
One person can learn and create. Start with one owner per creator workspace and a curated creator pilot; prevent cross-user and cross-workspace access from the first real backend slice. Teams and complex roles can follow a demonstrated need.

## Delivery sequence
A0 establishes a reviewed, explicitly local preview: discovery/search/category filters, course details, ordered public demo lessons, saved courses, progress/resume/notes and contextual posts/replies. It does not authenticate, charge, publish, notify or synchronize. Prototype limits and save failures remain visible.
A1 is the next integrated milestone, not an end-of-project backend task: verified accounts, creator ownership, draft/publication, idempotent free enrollment, protected lesson data, durable progress/notes and permissioned course discussions. Prove it with a creator and a separate learner in different browser sessions, including denied cross-account operations and conflict recovery.
A2 adds sandbox paid commerce and private media: a single course from one seller per order, server-owned prices, hosted checkout, verified idempotent fulfillment, refunds/access recovery, captions and protected delivery. Decide the merchant/marketplace model and supported regions before integrating real money.
A3 expands only after the core journey is dependable: creator profiles/spaces, course questions with accepted answers, targeted notifications, reviews, events, assessments or subscriptions. Product AI, gamification and leaderboards are not substitutes for a useful learning experience and require separate evaluation.

## Commercial and trust rules
A bookmark is not enrollment; a progress record is not an access grant; a redirect is not a receipt. Preserve source-specific grants and defined access/update terms. Ordinary unlisting stops new discovery/sales while preserving valid access; suspension is a separate safety action. Never invent testimonials, ratings, membership counts, creator credentials or earnings.
Before live selling resolve independent branding/assets, legal entity and supported buyer/seller markets, payment responsibilities, fees/taxes/refunds, access duration and update policy, privacy/retention/age rules, moderation/support ownership, limits and recovery. These are release decisions, not a reason to postpone local UI or a correctly isolated test backend.

## Success and scope control
Judge early work by whether real people can choose, start, resume and ask for help without assistance, and whether creators can publish without losing work. Track errors and recovery before vanity metrics. Each task must include a coherent entry-to-exit journey and failure states; avoid an inventory of impressive-looking dead screens.
`tasks.md` is the only completion ledger. `design.md` owns flow contracts; `style.md` owns visual continuity; `architecture.md` owns implementation boundaries. No provider, live community, production security certification or paid capability exists merely because this document describes it.
