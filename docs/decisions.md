## Deferred course defaults — recommendations, not implemented facts

A single multi-creator application with a consistent catalog, owned-course library and learning experience remains the product direction. Recommend a curated creator pilot, one owner per workspace, free enrollment and one-time one-course/one-seller purchases. Learner notes and contextual questions precede a broad community feed. Teams, subscriptions, carts, live/native/offline features and AI study tools need separate assignments.

Supabase, Stripe Connect/hosted Checkout, Mux and Vercel remain candidate service choices, not provisioned integrations or universal best options. Revalidate supported APIs, compatibility, costs, markets, rights and security at their implementation gate. Preserve small server-side authorization boundaries and avoid speculative infrastructure.

The later publishing default remains editable drafts, locked submissions and creator-locked published content, with attributable bounded operator corrections and stable lesson IDs. Payments and access remain separate, source-specific records. Ordinary unlisting preserves valid learner access; safety suspension is a distinct policy action.
