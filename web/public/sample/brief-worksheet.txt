A better brief - practice worksheet

This original worksheet is sample material, not paid course content.
Use fictional information. Do not include sensitive personal or business data.

1. Goal: What are you trying to make?

2. Audience: Who is this for?

3. Context: What constraints or background matter?

4. Format: What would a useful result look like?

5. Review: What assumptions need checking?

Write a first brief, compare it with these questions, and revise one thing.
